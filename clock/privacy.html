Clock Imperium for iPhone & iPad

Privacy policy.

Effective date: October 5, 2026

Clock Imperium runs on your device. The app does not collect or transmit personal information to the developer.

About this policy

This policy describes the Clock Imperium iPhone and iPad app, provided by Wesley Stevenson. It does not describe the separate Chess Imperium web game or its account features.

Information in the app

Clock Imperium does not require an account and contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. It does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or location.

Clock times, move counts, and time-control settings are held locally in memory while the app runs. The app does not upload them or maintain a server-side game history. Resetting a game clears its clock state and move counts; terminating the app clears its in-memory state.

Support email

If you email support, your email address and the information you choose to include will be received and used to respond to your request and resolve the issue. Email is processed by the email services used to send and receive the correspondence, including Gmail.

Support correspondence is kept only as long as needed to handle your request and any related follow-up, or to meet legal obligations. You may request deletion by emailing the address below. Support information is not sold or used for advertising.

Website links and Apple services

Opening a support or privacy link takes you to a website in your browser. The website hosting provider may process ordinary request information, such as an IP address, to deliver and secure the page. These support and privacy pages do not include analytics scripts, advertising, or cookies.

Apple independently processes information related to the App Store and any device diagnostics you choose to share under Apple’s own privacy settings and privacy policy.

Children’s privacy

The app does not collect personal information from users, including children. Please avoid including children’s personal information in support emails.

Changes to this policy

This page will be updated if the app’s privacy practices change. The effective date above identifies the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Wesley Stevenson at wesleylamontstevenson@gmail.com.
